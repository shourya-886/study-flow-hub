A warm bone field with near-black ink and one earth pigment used for emphasis and never as a fill. A high-contrast display serif does the talking at every size; everything factual is monospaced, small and widely tracked. Square corners throughout.

Spec

Apply a warm editorial studio style. Palette: ground #E7E3DA, secondary ground #DCD7CB, ink #1A1917, secondary ink #4A4741, muted #6E6A61, one pigment #9B3418, hairlines rgba(26,25,23,.16). Typography: 'Instrument Serif' roman and italic for all display and headings, letter-spacing -.015em, with the italic reserved for the emphasised phrase in the pigment; 'Azeret Mono' 400-600 at 10.5-12.5px with letter-spacing .15em-.17em, uppercase, for labels, and at 12.5px sentence case for body copy. Border-radius 0 everywhere, including buttons. MUST keep the pigment to type, a rule or a marker, never a filled area.
